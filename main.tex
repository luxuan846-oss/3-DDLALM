\begin{abstract}
We study a class of structured nonconvex constrained optimization problems with nonsmooth composite terms.  We develop a dual-damping linearized augmented Lagrangian method and analyze its approximate KKT residual guarantees.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
nonsmooth composite optimization, augmented Lagrangian method, dual-regularization, iteration complexity
