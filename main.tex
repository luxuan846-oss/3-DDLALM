\begin{abstract}
We study a class of structured nonconvex constrained optimization problems
with nonsmooth composite terms.  We develop a dual-damping linearized
augmented Lagrangian method and analyze its approximate KKT residual
guarantees.
\end{abstract}

augmented Lagrangian method, linearized ADMM, nonsmooth composite optimization, dual-regularization, iteration complexity
