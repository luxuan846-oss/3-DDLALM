## 结论

1. **移动中心下的下降闭合，中心步长为 $\alpha=c_\alpha\sqrt{\tau/s}$，$0\lt c_\alpha\le1/6$。** 参数沿用 Corollary G2.5，算法仍为 B2′，不需要修改算法或其余参数。【已证，Theorem G3.4】
2. **总势函数**为 $\mathcal W_k:=p_s(\mathbf z^k)+\frac12\mathcal E_k$，其中 $\mathcal E_k$ 是 Theorem G2.4 的能量在当前中心 $\mathbf z^k$ 处的取值。**一步不等式**（记 $\varsigma=\sqrt{\tau/s}$）：

$$
\mathcal W_{k+1}\le\mathcal W_k-\frac{c_\alpha\varsigma}{5\gamma}\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert^2-\frac{\varsigma}{4}\mathcal E_k-\frac{1}{16\tau}\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^{k+1}-\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k\rVert^2-\frac{\varsigma\mu_\gamma}{8}\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x_s^\star(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2-\frac{5\mu_\gamma}{192}\varpi_k^2 .
$$

3. **迫使 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma$ 的条件恰有两处，两处都只要求 $\alpha$ 与 $\varsigma$ 同阶。** (F1) 中心灵敏度的交叉项 $C_{\mathbf z,1}\sqrt{\mathcal E}\,\lVert\Delta_{\mathbf z}^{k+1}\rVert$ 只能用每步 $\varsigma$ 份额的压缩吸收，Young 不等式的代价 $C_{\mathbf z,1}^2\alpha^2\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert^2/\varsigma$ 必须小于 $p_s$ 的下降量 $\alpha\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert^2/(2\gamma)$。(F2) $p_s$ 的下降漏出 $\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x_s^\star(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2$，只能由 G2.4 中的 $\varsigma\Gamma_k^\star\ge\frac{\varsigma\mu_\gamma}2\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x_s^\star(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2$ 支付。常数 $C_{\mathbf z,1}^2=10/(3\gamma^2\mu_\gamma)$ 与 $\mu_\gamma$ 都不含 $s$，所以条件不会退化为 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma^2$ 或 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma/s$。【已证】两处条件对本势函数的必要性是推断（3.3 节）；对算法本身是否必要【待证】。
4. **$\sqrt s$ 的抵消。** 对偶距离项的权重 $1/(2s)$、$\lVert\mathbf w-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert\le\sqrt{2s\mathcal E}$、灵敏度 $\lVert\Delta\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert\le\sqrt s\lVert\Delta\mathbf z\rVert/(2\gamma\sqrt{\mu_\gamma})$ 的 $s$ 幂次之和为零。值间隙项 $p_s-d_s$ 关于中心的梯度是 $(\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z)-\mathbf x_s^\star(\mathbf z))/\gamma$，由 Lemma G2.2(ii) 以 $\sqrt{2(p_s-d_s)/\mu_\gamma}$ 控制，不经过 $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert$。**若换用 $L_\star\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert\le L_\star\sqrt{2s\mathcal E}$，$C_{\mathbf z,1}$ 多出因子 $\sqrt s$，(F1) 变成 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma/s$，加速失效。**【已证】
5. **$\mathcal W$ 的下界与初值都与 $s$ 无关。** $\mathcal W_k\ge\inf F-L_h^2/(2(\rho+s))$；零初值下 $\mathcal W_0-\inf_k\mathcal W_k\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}:=\Delta+L_h^2/\rho+(G_f+G_{\mathcal A}L_h)^2/(2\mu_\gamma)$，它不超过附录 C 的 $\bar\Delta$。对偶距离项的初值为 $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda_s^\star(\mathbf 0)\rVert^2/(2s)\le L_h^2/(2(\rho+s))$，依据是对所有中心成立的 $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda_s^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert\le sL_h/(\rho+s)$。【已证，Proposition G3.5】
6. **交给 G4 的求和量**为 $\sum_{k\lt K}\varsigma\left[\frac{c_\alpha}{5\gamma}\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert^2+\frac14\mathcal E_k\right]\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}$。在同一指标上，$\mathcal E_k$ 控制 $\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s$ 与对偶步长，$\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert$ 控制 KKT 原始残差中的中心项。取 $s=c_s/\epsilon$、$c_s\ge2\bar C_\lambda L_h$、$K\ge4C_R^2\Delta_{\mathcal W}\sqrt{c_s/\tau}\,\epsilon^{-5/2}$，前 $K$ 个输出中有一个满足 $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{KKT}}\le\epsilon$。【已证，Corollary G3.6；G4 需复核形式化细节，见第 6 节】

## 0. 设定、记号与 G2.4 的单步形式

### 0.1 参数与算法

**假设 (H).** $\gamma L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}\le\frac1{10}$，$\eta=\gamma$；$\tau\le\min\{\rho/4,\,1/(14\gamma G_{\mathcal A}^2)\}$；$s\ge\max\{\rho,4\tau\}$ 且在运行中固定；$\varsigma:=\sqrt{\tau/s}\le\frac12$，$\theta_{\mathrm m}:=(1-\varsigma)/(1+\varsigma)$；$\alpha:=c_\alpha\varsigma$，$0\lt c_\alpha\le\frac16$，于是 $\alpha\le\frac1{12}$。

前四条即 Corollary G2.5 的条件，最后一条是本文新增的唯一条件。B2′ 从 $(\mathbf x^0,\boldsymbol\lambda^0,\mathbf z^0)=\mathbf 0$、$\boldsymbol\lambda^{-1}=\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{-1}:=\boldsymbol\lambda^0$ 出发，对 $k\ge0$：

$$
\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k=\boldsymbol\lambda^k+\theta_{\mathrm m}(\boldsymbol\lambda^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^{k-1}),\qquad
\mathbf x^{k+1}=\mathbf x^k-\gamma\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x^k,\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k;\mathbf z^k,\mathbf 0),
$$

$$
\boldsymbol\lambda^{k+1}=\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k+\tau\nabla_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k;\mathbf z^k,\mathbf 0),\qquad
\mathbf z^{k+1}=\mathbf z^k+\alpha(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k).
$$

- 沿用 G2：$\mu:=\mu_\gamma=1/\gamma-L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}$，$L_x=1/\gamma+L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}$，$G:=G_{\mathcal A}$，$\upsilon:=\gamma L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}$，$L_\star:=G/\mu$，$c_e:=12\upsilon^2(L_x+\mu/2)$，$c_r:=G^2/(4\mu)$。新增 $G_0:=G_f+G_{\mathcal A}L_h$。

$$
\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z):=p(\mathbf z)-d(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z)+\frac1{2s}\lVert\mathbf w^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert^2+c_e\lVert\mathbf x^k-\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z)\rVert^2+c_r\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^k-\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1}\rVert^2,
\qquad
\mathcal E_k:=\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z^k).
$$

  $\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)$ 的四项都非负。$\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^k)$ 是第 $k$ 步之后、中心更新之前的能量，对应 Zhang–Xu (9.2) 中的 $\mathcal E_{z_t}(w_{t+1})$。
- 记号检索（`overleaf/` 下全部 `.tex`）：$\mathcal W$、$C_{\mathbf z,1}$、$C_{\mathbf z,2}$、$C_R$、$\Delta_{\mathcal W}$ 未使用；$\chi$ 在 `appendix/LowerBoundRotation.tex` 有局部含义；$c_\alpha$ 在附录 C 表示 $\alpha=c_\alpha\epsilon$ 的系数，此处表示 $\alpha=c_\alpha\varsigma$ 的系数。写入论文前需要换名。G2 已占用 $\mathcal V_k$（Nesterov 能量），所以总势函数用 $\mathcal W_k$。

### 0.4 G2.4 是任意状态上的单步不等式

**Lemma G3.0 (one-step form of Theorem G2.4).** Assume (H); the condition on $\alpha$ is not used. Fix $k\ge0$ and a center $\mathbf z\in\mathbb R^n$. Let $(\mathbf x^k,\boldsymbol\lambda^{k-1},\boldsymbol\lambda^k,\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1})$ be arbitrary, and let $\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k,\mathbf x^{k+1},\boldsymbol\lambda^{k+1}$ be produced by one B2′ step with center $\mathbf z$. Then

$$
\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z)\le(1-\varsigma)\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)-\frac1{8\tau}r_k^2-\frac{5\mu}{96}\varpi_k(\mathbf z)^2-\varsigma\Gamma_k^\star(\mathbf z),
$$

where $\varpi_k(\mathbf z)$ and $\Gamma_k^\star(\mathbf z)$ are the quantities of §0.2 with $\mathbf z^k$ replaced by $\mathbf z$. Moreover, Corollary G2.6 (i)–(v) hold with $\mathcal E_k$ replaced by $\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)$ and all value functions, minimizers and saddle points taken at $\mathbf z$. 【已证】

*Justification.* The proof of Theorem G2.4 uses only the following ingredients at step $k$: Lemma G2.1, which holds for arbitrary $\boldsymbol\lambda^{k-1},\boldsymbol\lambda^k,\mathbf x^{k+1}$; the pointwise bounds on $\Gamma_k^+$ and $\Gamma_k$, which use Lemma G2.2(i) and the Lipschitz continuity of $\mathbf x_s(\cdot;\mathbf z)$; Lemma G2.3 at step $k$, whose proof uses the gradient step from $\mathbf x^k$ at $\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k$ with center $\mathbf z$, the triangle inequality with the reference point $\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z)$, and $\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^k=\theta_{\mathrm m}(\boldsymbol\lambda^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^{k-1})$; and four coefficient inequalities that involve only parameters. None of them uses how $\mathbf x^k$ or $\boldsymbol\lambda^k$ were generated, in particular not the center used at step $k-1$. Corollary G2.6(i) uses the one-step inequality and $\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z)\ge0$; items (ii)–(v) are pointwise in the state. $\square$

说明。G3 只使用 G2.4 的最终不等式；G2.4 内部的带符号差 $\Gamma_k^+-(1-\varsigma)\Gamma_k$ 在 G3 中不出现，审查意见第 2 条的限制自动满足。B2′ 的第 $k$ 步使用中心 $\mathbf z^k$，所以取 $\mathbf z=\mathbf z^k$ 得到

$$
\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^k)\le(1-\varsigma)\mathcal E_k-\frac1{8\tau}r_k^2-\frac{5\mu}{96}\varpi_k^2-\varsigma\Gamma_k^\star .
$$

## 1. 外层值函数 $p_s$

**Lemma G3.1 (outer value function).** Let Assumptions `ass:smoothness` and `ass:h` hold, $s\gt0$, and $\gamma L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}\lt\frac12$ (the condition of `prop:curvature-smoothness`, under which Lemma G2.7 is stated). For $\mathbf z,\mathbf z'\in\mathbb R^n$ write $\boldsymbol\delta:=\mathbf z'-\mathbf z$.

- (i) $p(\mathbf z)=\min_{\mathbf x}\{\Phi_s(\mathbf x)+\frac1{2\gamma}\lVert\mathbf x-\mathbf z\rVert^2\}$ with unique minimizer $\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z)$, where $\Phi_s=\mathcal D_{\rho+s}(\cdot,\mathbf 0)=\max_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\{\mathcal D_\rho(\cdot,\boldsymbol\lambda)-\frac1{2s}\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda\rVert^2\}$. Moreover $F-\frac{L_h^2}{2(\rho+s)}\le\Phi_s\le F$ on $\mathbb R^n$, and

$$
\inf F-\frac{L_h^2}{2(\rho+s)}\le p(\mathbf z)\le F(\mathbf z).
$$

- (ii) With $\nabla p(\mathbf z):=(\mathbf z-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z))/\gamma$,

$$
-\left(\frac1{\gamma\mu}-\frac12\right)\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{\gamma}\le p(\mathbf z')-p(\mathbf z)-\langle\nabla p(\mathbf z),\boldsymbol\delta\rangle\le\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{2\gamma}.
$$

  Hence $p$ is differentiable with this gradient, and $\nabla p$ is $(1/\gamma)$-Lipschitz; this is `lem:v2-value-regularity`(iii) at $\boldsymbol\mu=\mathbf 0$, and the constant does not depend on $s$.
- (iii) For every $\boldsymbol\lambda$, the same two statements hold for $d(\boldsymbol\lambda;\cdot)$ with $\nabla_{\mathbf z}d(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z)=(\mathbf z-\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z))/\gamma$. In particular,

$$
d(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z')-d(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z)\ge\frac1\gamma\langle\mathbf z-\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z'),\boldsymbol\delta\rangle+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{2\gamma}.
$$

- (iv) $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert\le\frac{s}{\rho+s}L_h\le L_h$ for every $\mathbf z$.
- (v) $\nabla p(\mathbf z)=\nabla\Phi_s(\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z))=\nabla f(\mathbf x^\star)+J_{\mathcal A}(\mathbf x^\star)^\top\hat{\mathbf q}$, where $\hat{\mathbf y}:=\operatorname{prox}_{h/(\rho+s)}(\mathcal A(\mathbf x^\star))$, $\hat{\mathbf q}:=(\rho+s)(\mathcal A(\mathbf x^\star)-\hat{\mathbf y})\in\partial h(\hat{\mathbf y})$, and $\lVert\mathcal A(\mathbf x^\star)-\hat{\mathbf y}\rVert\le L_h/(\rho+s)$. Hence $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{KKT}}(\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z),\hat{\mathbf y},\hat{\mathbf q})\le\max\{\lVert\nabla p(\mathbf z)\rVert,L_h/(\rho+s)\}$.

【已证】

*Proof.* (i) By `eq:minimax-equality` and `eq:effective-penalty` with $\boldsymbol\mu=\mathbf 0$, $p(\mathbf z)=\min_{\mathbf x}\max_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z,\mathbf 0)=\min_{\mathbf x}\{\Phi_s(\mathbf x)+\lVert\mathbf x-\mathbf z\rVert^2/(2\gamma)\}$. The function $\mathbf x\mapsto\max_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z,\mathbf 0)$ is a pointwise maximum of $\mu$-strongly convex functions, hence $\mu$-strongly convex, and the primal component of the saddle point is its unique minimizer. Put $\sigma:=\rho+s$. By `eq:reduced-al` at $\boldsymbol\lambda=\mathbf 0$, $\mathcal D_\sigma(\mathbf x,\mathbf 0)=f(\mathbf x)+\min_{\mathbf y}\{h(\mathbf y)+\frac\sigma2\lVert\mathcal A(\mathbf x)-\mathbf y\rVert^2\}$. The choice $\mathbf y=\mathcal A(\mathbf x)$ gives $\Phi_s\le F$, and $h(\mathbf y)\ge h(\mathcal A(\mathbf x))-L_h\lVert\mathbf y-\mathcal A(\mathbf x)\rVert$ gives $\Phi_s\ge F-L_h^2/(2\sigma)$. Taking $\mathbf x=\mathbf z$ gives $p(\mathbf z)\le\Phi_s(\mathbf z)\le F(\mathbf z)$, and $p\ge\inf\Phi_s\ge\inf F-L_h^2/(2\sigma)$.

(ii) Write $\hat{\mathbf x}:=\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z)$ and $\hat{\mathbf x}':=\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z')$. Testing the minimization for $\mathbf z'$ with $\hat{\mathbf x}$,

$$
p(\mathbf z')-p(\mathbf z)\le\frac{\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}-\mathbf z'\rVert^2-\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}-\mathbf z\rVert^2}{2\gamma}=\frac1\gamma\langle\mathbf z-\hat{\mathbf x},\boldsymbol\delta\rangle+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{2\gamma}.
$$

Testing the minimization for $\mathbf z$ with $\hat{\mathbf x}'$,

$$
p(\mathbf z')-p(\mathbf z)\ge\frac{\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}'-\mathbf z'\rVert^2-\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}'-\mathbf z\rVert^2}{2\gamma}=\frac1\gamma\langle\mathbf z-\hat{\mathbf x}',\boldsymbol\delta\rangle+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{2\gamma}.
$$

Lemma G2.7 gives $\lvert\langle\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x},\boldsymbol\delta\rangle\rvert\le\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2/(\gamma\mu)$, which yields the lower bound. A two-sided $O(\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2)$ remainder gives Fréchet differentiability with gradient $\nabla p(\mathbf z)$. For the Lipschitz statement, the proof of Lemma G2.7 gives $\langle\boldsymbol\delta,\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x}\rangle\ge\gamma\mu\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x}\rVert^2=(1-\upsilon)\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x}\rVert^2$. Therefore

$$
\gamma^2\lVert\nabla p(\mathbf z')-\nabla p(\mathbf z)\rVert^2=\lVert\boldsymbol\delta-(\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x})\rVert^2\le\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2-(1-2\upsilon)\lVert\hat{\mathbf x}'-\hat{\mathbf x}\rVert^2\le\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2,
$$

because $\upsilon\lt\frac12$.

(iii) Repeat (ii) with $\Phi_s$ replaced by the $L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}$-weakly convex function $\mathcal D_\rho(\cdot,\boldsymbol\lambda)-\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda\rVert^2/(2s)$, whose proximal minimizer is $\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z)$, and use the third bound of Lemma G2.7. The displayed inequality is the lower estimate before Lemma G2.7 is applied. The Lipschitz statement follows from the $\mu$-strong monotonicity of $\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s(\cdot,\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z,\mathbf 0)$ and $\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z'),\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z,\mathbf 0)=\boldsymbol\delta/\gamma$.

(iv) $\boldsymbol\lambda^\star:=\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)$ maximizes the differentiable, $(1/s)$-strongly concave function $\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x^\star,\cdot;\mathbf z,\mathbf 0)$. By Lemma G0.1, $\mathbf 0=(\mathbf q(\mathbf x^\star,\boldsymbol\lambda^\star)-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star)/\rho-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star/s$, so $\boldsymbol\lambda^\star=\frac{s}{\rho+s}\mathbf q(\mathbf x^\star,\boldsymbol\lambda^\star)$ and $\lVert\mathbf q\rVert\le L_h$.

(v) $\Phi_s=f+e_\sigma\circ\mathcal A$ with the Moreau envelope $e_\sigma$ of $h$, so $\nabla\Phi_s(\mathbf x)=\nabla f(\mathbf x)+J_{\mathcal A}(\mathbf x)^\top\sigma(\mathcal A(\mathbf x)-\operatorname{prox}_{h/\sigma}(\mathcal A(\mathbf x)))$, as in Lemma G0.1 with $(\rho,\boldsymbol\lambda)=(\sigma,\mathbf 0)$. The optimality condition of $\hat{\mathbf x}$ in (i) is $\nabla\Phi_s(\hat{\mathbf x})+(\hat{\mathbf x}-\mathbf z)/\gamma=\mathbf 0$, i.e. $\nabla\Phi_s(\hat{\mathbf x})=\nabla p(\mathbf z)$. The prox optimality condition gives $\hat{\mathbf q}\in\partial h(\hat{\mathbf y})$, so $\lVert\hat{\mathbf q}\rVert\le L_h$ and $\lVert\mathcal A(\hat{\mathbf x})-\hat{\mathbf y}\rVert=\lVert\hat{\mathbf q}\rVert/\sigma\le L_h/\sigma$. $\square$

讨论。

- $p$ 是二次罚函数 $\Phi_s=\mathcal D_{\rho+s}(\cdot,\mathbf 0)$ 的 Moreau 包络（参数 $\gamma$）。这里的 $\Phi_s$ 与附录 C Step 4 的 $\Phi_s$ 是同一个函数：附录 C 在 $\mathcal Y$ 上取最大，由 `eq:beta0-perturbed-objective` 与全空间上的最大值相等。**$\Phi_s$ 自身的光滑常数含 $(\rho+s)G_{\mathcal A}^2$，随 $s$ 增长；$p$ 的光滑常数 $1/\gamma$ 与 $s$ 无关**，因为它只依赖原始强凸模数 $\mu_\gamma$。【已证】
- Zhang–Xu (3.2)、(8.3) 给出 $\nabla p=2L(z-x^\star(z))$ 与 $6L$-Lipschitz，$2L$ 对应这里的 $1/\gamma$。他们用 (8.2) 的系数 2 得到 $6L$；用强单调不等式的完整形式可得 $1/\gamma$。
- (v) 在 G3 中不使用。B2′ 的输出取在 $(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)$，由 Corollary G3.6 处理；(v) 只说明 $\nabla p$ 小时 $\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z)$ 是原问题的近似 KKT 点，可行性偏差为 $L_h/(\rho+s)=O(\epsilon)$。

## 2. 中心移动误差

**Lemma G3.2 (center-movement error).** Assume $\gamma L_{\mathcal D,\mathbf x}\le\frac1{10}$ and $s\gt0$. Fix $k$ and the state $(\mathbf x^k,\boldsymbol\lambda^{k-1},\boldsymbol\lambda^k,\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1})$, and let $\mathbf z,\mathbf z'\in\mathbb R^n$, $\boldsymbol\delta:=\mathbf z'-\mathbf z$. Then

$$
\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z')-\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)\le C_{\mathbf z,1}\sqrt{\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)}\,\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert+C_{\mathbf z,2}\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2,
\qquad
C_{\mathbf z,1}:=\frac{1}{\gamma}\sqrt{\frac{10}{3\mu}},\qquad C_{\mathbf z,2}:=\frac{4}{3\gamma^2\mu}.
$$

The constants do not depend on $s$, $\tau$, $\varsigma$, $\rho$, or the state. 【已证】

*Proof.* Write $A_1:=p(\mathbf z)-d(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z)$, $A_2:=\frac1{2s}\lVert\mathbf w^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert^2$, $A_3:=c_e\lVert\mathbf x^k-\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z)\rVert^2$. The $c_r$ term does not depend on the center, by (P3) of G2. So $A_1+A_2+A_3\le\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)$ and only three terms change.

Step 1 (value gap). By the upper estimate in Lemma G3.1(ii) and the displayed lower estimate in Lemma G3.1(iii) with $\boldsymbol\lambda=\boldsymbol\lambda^k$, the two terms $\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2/(2\gamma)$ cancel:

$$
\left[p(\mathbf z')-d(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z')\right]-\left[p(\mathbf z)-d(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z)\right]\le\frac1\gamma\langle\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z')-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z),\boldsymbol\delta\rangle\le\frac{\lVert\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z)-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert}{\gamma}\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{\gamma^2\mu},
$$

where the last step uses the third bound of Lemma G2.7. Lemma G2.2(ii) at center $\mathbf z$ gives $\lVert\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda^k;\mathbf z)-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert\le\sqrt{2A_1/\mu}$.

Step 2 (dual distance). Let $\boldsymbol\Delta:=\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z')-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)$. Lemma G2.7 gives $\lVert\boldsymbol\Delta\rVert^2\le s\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2/(4\gamma^2\mu)$, and $\lVert\mathbf w^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert=\sqrt{2sA_2}$. Hence

$$
\frac1{2s}\left(\lVert\mathbf w^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z')\rVert^2-\lVert\mathbf w^k-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf z)\rVert^2\right)\le\frac1s\sqrt{2sA_2}\,\frac{\sqrt s\,\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert}{2\gamma\sqrt\mu}+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{8\gamma^2\mu}=\sqrt{\frac{A_2}{2}}\,\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert}{\gamma\sqrt\mu}+\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{8\gamma^2\mu}.
$$

Step 3 (tracking). Let $\boldsymbol\Delta':=\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z')-\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z)$, so $\lVert\boldsymbol\Delta'\rVert\le\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert/(\gamma\mu)$ by Lemma G2.7. Expanding the square,

$$
c_e\left(\lVert\mathbf x^k-\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z')\rVert^2-\lVert\mathbf x^k-\mathbf x_s(\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{k-1};\mathbf z)\rVert^2\right)\le2\sqrt{\frac{c_e}{\mu}}\sqrt{A_3}\,\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert}{\gamma\sqrt\mu}+\frac{c_e}{\mu}\,\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2}{\gamma^2\mu}.
$$

Step 4 (sum). The linear terms add up to $\frac{\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert}{\gamma\sqrt\mu}\left[\sqrt2\sqrt{A_1}+\frac1{\sqrt2}\sqrt{A_2}+2\sqrt{c_e/\mu}\sqrt{A_3}\right]$. By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality the bracket is at most $\sqrt{5/2+4c_e/\mu}\,\sqrt{A_1+A_2+A_3}\le\sqrt{10/3}\,\sqrt{\mathcal E_k(\mathbf z)}$ by (N3). The quadratic terms add up to $(1+\frac18+\frac{c_e}\mu)\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2/(\gamma^2\mu)\le\frac43\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert^2/(\gamma^2\mu)$ by (N3). $\square$

讨论。

- **$s$ 幂次核对。** Step 2 的线性项是 $s^{-1}\cdot s^{1/2}\cdot s^{1/2}=s^0$：权重、能量控制的距离、鞍点灵敏度各贡献一个因子。二次项是 $s^{-1}\cdot s=s^0$。Step 1 与 Step 3 不含 $s$。G2 第 3 节末尾的预估与此一致。【已证】
- **Step 1 是唯一需要选择的地方。** 值间隙的 $\mathbf z$-梯度是 $(\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda;\mathbf z)-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z))/\gamma$。若用 (P2) 写成 $L_\star\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert/\gamma$ 再用 $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert\le\sqrt{2s\mathcal E}$，线性系数变为 $\Theta(L_\star\sqrt s/\gamma)$，后文条件 (F1) 变为 $c_\alpha\lesssim1/s$，即 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma/s$，比附录 C 的 $\alpha\propto1/s$ 还小。Lemma G2.2(ii) 的第二项 $\frac\mu2\lVert\mathbf x_s(\boldsymbol\lambda)-\mathbf x^\star\rVert^2$ 给出 $\Theta(1)$ 控制，避开了这一点。【已证】
- **与 Zhang–Xu 的对应。** 这是他们的 Lemma 9.3 (9.57)：线性系数 $2L\lVert x-x^\star(z)\rVert+4\lVert\nabla_xG+\xi\rVert+\frac18\lVert v\rVert+\frac{\sqrt{2L\mu_y}}{128}\lVert y-y^\star(z)\rVert$，二次系数 $\frac{1025L}{128}$。任务书称作"Lemma 9.4（中心移动误差）"的内容，在原文中分为 Lemma 9.3（误差估计）与 Lemma 9.4（误差与固定中心耗散的合并）。

## 3. 总势函数的一步下降

### 3.1 中心步上的 $p$

**Lemma G3.3 (center step).** For every $k\ge0$ and $\alpha\in[0,1]$,

$$
p(\mathbf z^{k+1})-p(\mathbf z^k)\le-\frac{\alpha(1-\alpha)}{2\gamma}\chi_k^2+\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}(\chi_k^\star)^2 .
$$

【已证】*Proof.* Apply the upper estimate of Lemma G3.1(ii) with $\boldsymbol\delta=\alpha(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k)$. Since $\mathbf z^k-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z^k)=(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z^k))-(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k)$, this is `eq:z-step-p` at $\boldsymbol\mu=\mathbf 0$:

$$
p(\mathbf z^{k+1})-p(\mathbf z^k)\le-\frac{\alpha(2-\alpha)}{2\gamma}\chi_k^2+\frac\alpha\gamma\langle\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z^k),\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rangle .
$$

Young's inequality $\langle\mathbf a,\mathbf b\rangle\le\frac12\lVert\mathbf a\rVert^2+\frac12\lVert\mathbf b\rVert^2$ on the inner product gives the claim. $\square$

Lemma G3.1(ii) 的上界不需要 $\nabla p$ 的 Lipschitz 性，所以本引理只用到 $\gamma\mu\gt0$。

### 3.2 主定理

**Theorem G3.4 (one-step descent of the total potential).** Assume (H). Define $\mathcal W_k:=p(\mathbf z^k)+\frac12\mathcal E_k$. Then, for every $k\ge0$,

$$
\mathcal W_{k+1}\le\mathcal W_k-\frac{\alpha}{5\gamma}\chi_k^2-\frac\varsigma4\mathcal E_k-\frac1{16\tau}r_k^2-\frac{\varsigma\mu}{8}(\chi_k^\star)^2-\frac{5\mu}{192}\varpi_k^2 ,
\qquad \alpha=c_\alpha\varsigma .
$$

Since $\chi_k^\star\ge0$ and $\gamma^2\lVert\nabla p(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2\le2\chi_k^2+2(\chi_k^\star)^2$, it also gives

$$
\mathcal W_{k+1}\le\mathcal W_k-\frac{\alpha\gamma}{10}\lVert\nabla p(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2-\frac\varsigma4\mathcal E_k .
$$

【已证】

*Proof.* Step 1 (split, as in Zhang–Xu (9.1)–(9.2)).

$$
\mathcal W_{k+1}-\mathcal W_k=\left[p(\mathbf z^{k+1})-p(\mathbf z^k)\right]+\frac12\left[\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^k)-\mathcal E_k\right]+\frac12\left[\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^{k+1})-\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^k)\right].
$$

Step 2 (fixed center). Write $\tilde{\mathcal E}:=\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^k)\ge0$ and $\mathcal T_k:=\frac1{8\tau}r_k^2+\frac{5\mu}{96}\varpi_k^2+\varsigma\Gamma_k^\star\ge0$. Lemma G3.0 with $\mathbf z=\mathbf z^k$ gives $\tilde{\mathcal E}\le(1-\varsigma)\mathcal E_k-\mathcal T_k$, and Lemma G2.2(iii) gives $\Gamma_k^\star\ge\frac\mu2(\chi_k^\star)^2$.

Step 3 (moving center). Lemma G3.2 for the state $k+1$ with $\mathbf z=\mathbf z^k$, $\mathbf z'=\mathbf z^{k+1}$, $\lVert\boldsymbol\delta\rVert=\alpha\chi_k$, followed by $ab\le\frac\varsigma2a^2+\frac1{2\varsigma}b^2$ with $a=\sqrt{\tilde{\mathcal E}}$, gives

$$
\mathcal E_{k+1}(\mathbf z^{k+1})\le\left(1+\frac\varsigma2\right)\tilde{\mathcal E}+\left(\frac{C_{\mathbf z,1}^2}{2\varsigma}+C_{\mathbf z,2}\right)\alpha^2\chi_k^2 .
$$

Because $(1+\frac\varsigma2)(1-\varsigma)\le1-\frac\varsigma2$ and $1+\frac\varsigma2\ge1$, Step 2 yields

$$
\mathcal E_{k+1}-\mathcal E_k\le-\frac\varsigma2\mathcal E_k-\mathcal T_k+\left(\frac{C_{\mathbf z,1}^2}{2\varsigma}+C_{\mathbf z,2}\right)\alpha^2\chi_k^2 .
$$

Step 4 (combination). Add half of this inequality to Lemma G3.3:

$$
\mathcal W_{k+1}-\mathcal W_k\le-\frac\varsigma4\mathcal E_k-\frac{1}{16\tau}r_k^2-\frac{5\mu}{192}\varpi_k^2-\left(\frac{\varsigma\mu}{4}-\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\right)(\chi_k^\star)^2-\frac\alpha\gamma\,b_k\,\chi_k^2,
$$

$$
b_k:=\frac{1-\alpha}{2}-\frac{\gamma\alpha}{2}\left(\frac{C_{\mathbf z,1}^2}{2\varsigma}+C_{\mathbf z,2}\right)=\frac{1-\alpha}2-\frac{5c_\alpha}{6\gamma\mu}-\frac{2c_\alpha\varsigma}{3\gamma\mu},
$$

where $C_{\mathbf z,1}^2=10/(3\gamma^2\mu)$, $C_{\mathbf z,2}=4/(3\gamma^2\mu)$ and $\alpha=c_\alpha\varsigma$ were substituted. Two coefficient checks remain, using (N1), $c_\alpha\le\frac16$, $\varsigma\le\frac12$, $\alpha\le\frac1{12}$.

- (F2) Coefficient of $(\chi_k^\star)^2$: $\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}=\frac{c_\alpha\varsigma\mu}{2\gamma\mu}\le\frac{\varsigma\mu}{12\cdot0.9}\le\frac{\varsigma\mu}{8}$, so $\frac{\varsigma\mu}4-\frac\alpha{2\gamma}\ge\frac{\varsigma\mu}8$.
- (F1) Coefficient of $\chi_k^2$: $b_k\ge\frac{11}{24}-\frac{5}{36\cdot0.9}-\frac{1}{6\cdot2.7}\ge0.2422\ge\frac15$.

This proves the first inequality. For the second, $\frac{\alpha}{5\gamma}\le\frac{\varsigma}{30\gamma}\le\frac{\varsigma\mu}8$ by (N1), so $\frac\alpha{5\gamma}\chi_k^2+\frac{\varsigma\mu}8(\chi_k^\star)^2\ge\frac{\alpha}{5\gamma}(\chi_k^2+(\chi_k^\star)^2)\ge\frac{\alpha\gamma}{10}\lVert\nabla p(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2$, using $\gamma\nabla p(\mathbf z^k)=(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z^k))-(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k)$. $\square$

### 3.3 哪个条件迫使 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma$

- **(F1) 交叉项。** 中心移动误差的线性部分 $C_{\mathbf z,1}\sqrt{\tilde{\mathcal E}}\,\alpha\chi_k$ 只能对 $\tilde{\mathcal E}$ 用 Young 不等式，因为能量每步只多压缩 $\varsigma$ 份额；代价为 $C_{\mathbf z,1}^2\alpha^2\chi_k^2/(2\varsigma)$。它必须小于 $p$ 的下降 $\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}\chi_k^2$，即 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma/(\gamma C_{\mathbf z,1}^2)=\Theta(\varsigma)$。对一般权重 $\mathcal W_k=p(\mathbf z^k)+c_V\mathcal E_k$，该条件为 $c_Vc_\alpha\le\frac1{11}$。【已证】
- **(F2) 中心步的漏项。** $p$ 的下降含正项 $\frac{\alpha}{2\gamma}(\chi_k^\star)^2$。G2.4 对 $\chi_k^\star$ 的耗散只有 $\varsigma\Gamma_k^\star\ge\frac{\varsigma\mu}2(\chi_k^\star)^2$，所以需要 $\alpha\le c_V\gamma\mu\varsigma/2$。对一般 $c_V$，该条件为 $c_\alpha\le\frac9{20}c_V$。【已证】
- 两个条件同时成立的最大 $c_\alpha$ 为 $\max_{c_V}\min\{\frac9{20}c_V,\frac1{11c_V}\}=\sqrt{9/220}\approx0.20$；本文取 $c_V=\frac12$、$c_\alpha\le\frac16$。**两个条件都是 $\alpha$ 关于 $\varsigma$ 的线性约束，常数与 $s$ 无关。**其余各项（$C_{\mathbf z,2}\alpha^2\chi_k^2$、$\alpha^2\chi_k^2/(2\gamma)$）只要求 $\alpha=O(1)$。【已证】
- **与附录 C 的比较。** 附录 C 的 $\alpha=\kappa/(\ell s)$ 来自 Li26 的条件 $\alpha\le1/(384r\tau\omega_s^2)$ 与 $\omega_s^2=\Theta(s)$，即中心步长与非加速对偶块的压缩率 $\tau/s$ 同阶。这里中心步长与加速后的压缩率 $\varsigma=\sqrt{\tau/s}$ 同阶，二者之比 $\sqrt{s/\tau}$ 就是复杂度从 $\epsilon^{-3}$ 降到 $\epsilon^{-5/2}$ 的来源。【已证，指参数阶的对照】
- **必要性（推断）。** 若 $\alpha\gg\varsigma$，稳态下 $\sqrt{\mathcal E}\approx C_{\mathbf z,1}\alpha\chi/\varsigma$，而 $p$ 的下降要求 $\chi^\star\lesssim\chi$，即 $\sqrt{\mathcal E}\lesssim\sqrt\mu\,\chi$，于是需要 $\alpha\lesssim\varsigma$。按这一推断，对 $\mathcal W$ 这类"外层值函数加内层能量"的势函数，$\alpha=O(\varsigma)$ 是必要的【待证，推断】。B2′ 本身在 $\alpha\gg\varsigma$ 时是否仍有 $\epsilon^{-5/2}$：【待证】。这对上界无影响。

## 4. 交给 G4 的量

**Proposition G3.5 (lower bound and initial value).** Assume (H).

- (a) $\mathcal W_k\ge p(\mathbf z^k)\ge\inf F-\frac{L_h^2}{2(\rho+s)}$ for every $k$.
- (b) At the zero initialization,

$$
\mathcal E_0\le\frac{L_h^2}{2\rho}+\frac{L_h^2}{2(\rho+s)}+\left(\frac12+\frac{31}{150}\right)\frac{G_0^2}{\mu},
\qquad
\mathcal W_0-\inf_k\mathcal W_k\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}:=\Delta+\frac{L_h^2}{\rho}+\frac{G_0^2}{2\mu}\le\bar\Delta,
$$

  where $\bar\Delta=\Delta+L_h^2/\rho+G_0^2/\mu_\gamma$ is the constant of `eq:beta0-initial-gap`. Both bounds are independent of $s$, $\tau$, and $\epsilon$.

【已证】

*Proof.* (a) Lemma G3.1(i) and $\mathcal E_k\ge0$.

(b) With $\mathbf x^0=\mathbf z^0=\boldsymbol\lambda^0=\boldsymbol\lambda^{-1}=\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^{-1}=\mathbf 0$, we have $\mathbf w^0=\mathbf 0$ and the $c_r$ term vanishes. First, $p(\mathbf 0)\le F(\mathbf 0)$ by Lemma G3.1(i), and $d(\mathbf 0;\mathbf 0)=d_0:=\min_{\mathbf x}\{\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf x,\mathbf 0)+\lVert\mathbf x\rVert^2/(2\gamma)\}$. The objective of $d_0$ is $\mu$-strongly convex, so it is at least $v_0+\langle\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0),\mathbf x\rangle+\frac\mu2\lVert\mathbf x\rVert^2\ge v_0-G_0^2/(2\mu)$, with $v_0:=\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0)\ge F(\mathbf 0)-L_h^2/(2\rho)$ by the argument of Lemma G3.1(i) with $\sigma=\rho$, and $\lVert\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0)\rVert\le G_0$ by Lemma G0.1. Hence $p(\mathbf 0)-d(\mathbf 0;\mathbf 0)\le L_h^2/(2\rho)+G_0^2/(2\mu)$. Second, Lemma G3.1(iv) gives $\frac1{2s}\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^\star(\mathbf 0)\rVert^2\le\frac{sL_h^2}{2(\rho+s)^2}\le\frac{L_h^2}{2(\rho+s)}$. Third, $\mu\lVert\mathbf x_s(\mathbf 0;\mathbf 0)\rVert\le\lVert\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0;\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0)\rVert=\lVert\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf 0,\mathbf 0)\rVert\le G_0$, so $c_e\lVert\mathbf x_s(\mathbf 0;\mathbf 0)\rVert^2\le\frac{c_e}{\mu}\frac{G_0^2}{\mu}\le\frac{31}{150}\frac{G_0^2}\mu$ by (N2). This proves the bound on $\mathcal E_0$. Then, by (a), $\mathcal W_0\le F(\mathbf 0)+\frac12\mathcal E_0$, $F(\mathbf 0)-\inf F\le\Delta$ and $\rho+s\ge2\rho$,

$$
\mathcal W_0-\inf_k\mathcal W_k\le\Delta+\frac{L_h^2}{4\rho}+\frac{3L_h^2}{4(\rho+s)}+0.36\,\frac{G_0^2}{\mu}\le\Delta+\frac{5L_h^2}{8\rho}+0.36\,\frac{G_0^2}{\mu}\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}. \qquad\square
$$

说明：对偶距离项 $\lVert\mathbf w^0-\boldsymbol\lambda^\star\rVert^2/(2s)$ 在零初值下为 $O(1/s)$。若初值带动量（$\boldsymbol\lambda^0\ne\boldsymbol\lambda^{-1}$），$\mathbf w^0$ 含 $\varsigma^{-1}(\boldsymbol\lambda^0-\boldsymbol\lambda^{-1})$，该项为 $\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^0-\boldsymbol\lambda^{-1}\rVert^2/(2\tau)$ 量级，仍为 $O(1)$。B2′ 采用零初值，不涉及这一情形。【已证】

**Corollary G3.6 (summed descent and the output residual).** Assume (H) and let $K\ge1$.

- (i) Summed descent:

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}\left[\frac{c_\alpha\varsigma}{5\gamma}\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k\rVert^2+\frac\varsigma4\mathcal E_k+\frac1{16\tau}\lVert\boldsymbol\lambda^{k+1}-\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k\rVert^2+\frac{\varsigma\mu}8\lVert\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf x^\star(\mathbf z^k)\rVert^2\right]\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}.
$$

- (ii) Some $0\le k\lt K$ satisfies $M_k:=\frac{c_\alpha}{5\gamma}\chi_k^2+\frac14\mathcal E_k\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}/(\varsigma K)$, and for that $k$ the B2′ output $(\mathbf x^{k+1},\mathbf y(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k),\mathbf q(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k))$ satisfies

$$
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{KKT}}\le C_R\sqrt{\frac{\Delta_{\mathcal W}}{\varsigma K}}+\frac{\bar C_\lambda L_h}{s},
\qquad
C_R:=\max\left\{L_x\sqrt{\frac{7}{3\mu}}+\sqrt{\frac{5}{c_\alpha\gamma}},\ \sqrt{\frac{32}{\tau}}\right\}.
$$

- (iii) Let $s=c_s/\epsilon$ with $c_s\ge2\bar C_\lambda L_h$ and $\epsilon\le c_s/\max\{\rho,4\tau\}$. If

$$
K\ge4C_R^2\,\Delta_{\mathcal W}\sqrt{\frac{c_s}{\tau}}\;\epsilon^{-5/2},
$$

  then some output among the first $K$ satisfies $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{KKT}}\le\epsilon$. These $K$ iterations use $3K+1$ oracle calls.

【已证】

*Proof.* (i) Sum Theorem G3.4 over $k\lt K$ and use $\mathcal W_0-\mathcal W_K\le\Delta_{\mathcal W}$ (Proposition G3.5).

(ii) Drop the last two sums in (i) and divide by $\varsigma$; the minimum over $k$ is at most the average. At the chosen $k$, $\mathcal E_k\le4M_k$ and $\chi_k^2\le5\gamma M_k/c_\alpha$. Lemma G0.2 gives $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{KKT}}=\max\{\lVert\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)\rVert,\lVert\nabla_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)\rVert\}$. Primal part: Corollary G2.6(v) at $\mathbf z=\mathbf z^k$ gives $\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)=\nabla_{\mathbf x}\mathcal P_s(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k;\mathbf z^k,\mathbf 0)-(\mathbf x^{k+1}-\mathbf z^k)/\gamma$; Lemma G3.0 with Corollary G2.6(ii) bounds the first norm by $L_x\sqrt{7\mathcal E_k/(12\mu)}\le L_x\sqrt{7M_k/(3\mu)}$, and the second is $\chi_k/\gamma\le\sqrt{5M_k/(c_\alpha\gamma)}$. Dual part: Lemma G0.2 gives $\nabla_{\boldsymbol\lambda}\mathcal D_\rho(\mathbf x^{k+1},\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)=(\boldsymbol\lambda^{k+1}-\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k)/\tau+\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k/s$; Corollary G2.6(i) gives $r_k^2\le8\tau\mathcal E_k\le32\tau M_k$; Proposition G1.4 gives $\lVert\bar{\boldsymbol\lambda}^k\rVert\le\bar C_\lambda L_h$, applicable because the dual recursion of B2′ is that of B2, $\theta_{\mathrm m}$ is constant, and $\nu=\tau/\rho+\tau/s\le\frac12$ under (H).

(iii) $\varsigma=\sqrt{\tau\epsilon/c_s}$, so the condition on $K$ gives $\Delta_{\mathcal W}/(\varsigma K)\le\epsilon^2/(4C_R^2)$ and the first term in (ii) is at most $\epsilon/2$; the second is at most $\epsilon/2$ by the choice of $c_s$. The condition on $\epsilon$ gives $s\ge\max\{\rho,4\tau\}$. The oracle count is Proposition G0.3, which applies to B2′ as noted in G2 §0. $\square$

待证：
