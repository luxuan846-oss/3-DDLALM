\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
In this paper, we study single-loop linearized ALMs with a fixed penalty for
the nonsmooth nonconvex composite Problem~\eqref{prob:main}. Within the LRALM
family \(\calC_\theta\), matching upper and lower bounds show that the best
worst-case complexity for finding an \(\epsilon\)-KKT point is
\(\Theta(\epsilon^{-4})\) whenever the effective penalty remains below a
threshold of order \(\epsilon^{-1}\), and \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})\) for a fixed
penalty \(\rho\) with dual regularization \(s=\Theta(\epsilon^{-1})\). Thus,
dual regularization of order \(\epsilon^{-1}\) suffices for a fixed-penalty
member of \(\calC_\theta\) to attain \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})\), yet no member of
the family improves on this rate. Beyond \(\calC_\theta\), Fast LRALM uses a
projection-free Nesterov-accelerated dual update and attains
\(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) iteration complexity with a fixed penalty. This
result establishes a complexity separation from \(\calC_\theta\) and matches the
optimal rate for smooth NC-C minimax problems~\cite{pan2026optimal}. An
interesting direction for future research is to determine whether
\(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) is optimal for Problem~\eqref{prob:main} and
whether accuracy-independent parameter schedules can achieve the same rate.
