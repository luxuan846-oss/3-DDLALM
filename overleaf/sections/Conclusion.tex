\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper studies LRALM, a single-loop linearized regularized augmented
Lagrangian framework for the structured nonsmooth nonconvex composite
problem~\eqref{prob:main}. Within the constant-stepsize family \(\calC_\theta\),
we establish tight worst-case complexity bounds for finding an
\(\epsilon\)-KKT point, obtaining \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-4})\) whenever
\(\rho+s\le c_1/\epsilon\) and \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})\) for fixed \(\rho\) and
\(s=c_s/\epsilon\) with sufficiently large \(c_s\). Our analysis shows that the
complexity order of \(\calC_\theta\) is governed by the effective penalty
\(\rho+s\) uniformly across all constant center stepsizes, so the dual
regularization alone can carry the required \(1/\epsilon\) growth while keeping
the penalty \(\rho\) fixed. Fixing the dual center at zero, we further develop a novel algorithm 
Fast LRALM with one Nesterov extrapolation step on the dual variable and
establish its \(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) iteration complexity. To the best of our knowledge, Fast LRALM is the first single-loop
linearized ALM that finds an \(\epsilon\)-KKT point of
Problem~\eqref{prob:main} in \(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) iterations
with a fixed penalty parameter.
