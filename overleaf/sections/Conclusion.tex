\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper studies the LRALM family \(\calC_\theta\) for the structured
nonsmooth nonconvex composite Problem~\eqref{prob:main}. Within
\(\calC_\theta\), we establish tight worst-case complexity bounds for finding
an \(\epsilon\)-KKT point, obtaining \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-4})\) whenever
\(\rho+s\le c_1/\epsilon\) and \(\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})\) for a fixed penalty
\(\rho\) and dual regularization \(s=c_s/\epsilon\) with sufficiently large
\(c_s\). These results delineate the complementary roles of \(\rho\) and \(s\).
Because the complexity order of \(\calC_\theta\) is governed by the effective
penalty \(\rho+s\) uniformly across all constant center stepsizes, \(s\) alone
can carry the required \(1/\epsilon\) growth while keeping \(\rho\) and the
primal smoothness modulus fixed. Moving beyond \(\calC_\theta\) with the dual center anchored at zero, we
develop Fast LRALM by adding Nesterov extrapolation to the dual update and
prove that it attains \(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) iteration complexity with
a fixed penalty, matching, to the best of our knowledge, the fastest multi-loop
rate for this problem class without inner solvers or dual projections. An
interesting direction for future research is to
determine whether \(\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})\) is optimal for
Problem~\eqref{prob:main} and whether accuracy-independent parameter schedules
can achieve the same rate.
