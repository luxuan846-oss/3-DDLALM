\newcommand{\papertitle}{Accelerated Linearized Augmented Lagrangian
Method with a Fixed Penalty for Nonconvex Composite Optimization}
\newcommand{\paperkeywords}{augmented Lagrangian method, nonconvex composite
optimization, iteration complexity, lower bound, dual regularization,
gradient descent--ascent}
\newcommand{\papermsc}{65K05, 90C26, 90C30, 90C46, 90C60}
\newcommand{\paperabstract}{%
We study single-loop linearized augmented Lagrangian methods (ALMs) for the
nonconvex composite problem. Existing single-loop guarantees reach $\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-3})$ iteration complexity only by
taking a large penalty $\rho=\Theta(\epsilon^{-1})$, which inflates the primal
smoothness modulus and shrinks the stepsize. To keep $\rho$ fixed, we analyze a
family of linearized regularized ALMs (LRALM) parameterized by $\rho$, a dual
regularization parameter $s$, and moving primal and dual centers; here the
primal smoothness depends only on $\rho$, whereas $\rho+s$ acts as an effective
penalty. Combining upper bounds with hard-instance lower bounds for finding an
$\epsilon$-KKT point, we obtain a sharp dichotomy within LRALM: the tight
complexity is $\Theta(\epsilon^{-4})$ whenever $\rho+s$ stays below a constant
multiple of $\epsilon^{-1}$, and improves to $\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})$ when
$\rho=\Theta(1)$ and $s=\Theta(\epsilon^{-1})$. Stepping outside LRALM, we
propose Fast LRALM, which adds Nesterov extrapolation to the dual update and
attains $\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})$ complexity with a fixed penalty, matching
the best known multi-loop rate without inner solvers or dual projections.
}
