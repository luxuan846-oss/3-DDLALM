\newcommand{\papertitle}{Accelerated Linearized Augmented Lagrangian
Method with a Fixed Penalty for Nonconvex Composite Optimization}
\newcommand{\paperkeywords}{nonconvex composite optimization, augmented
Lagrangian method, single-loop method, fixed penalty, dual regularization,
lower complexity bound}
\newcommand{\papermsc}{90C26, 90C30, 90C47, 90C60, 65K05, 49M37}
\newcommand{\paperabstract}{%
In this paper, we study single-loop linearized augmented Lagrangian methods
(ALMs) for nonconvex composite optimization. Existing single-loop penalty and
linearized ALM methods typically attain $\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-3})$ complexity
by scaling the penalty parameter as $\Theta(\epsilon^{-1})$, which inflates the
primal smoothness modulus and shrinks the primal stepsize. To keep the penalty
fixed, we study a family of linearized regularized ALMs (LRALM) with dual
regularization and moving centers, where dual regularization raises the
effective penalty without increasing primal smoothness. Combining upper bounds
with matching hard-instance lower bounds for finding an $\epsilon$-KKT point,
we prove that the tight complexity within LRALM is $\Theta(\epsilon^{-4})$
whenever the effective penalty remains below a threshold of order
$\epsilon^{-1}$, and improves to $\Theta(\epsilon^{-3})$ with a fixed penalty
and $\Theta(\epsilon^{-1})$ dual regularization. Beyond this family, we develop
Fast LRALM by adding Nesterov extrapolation to the dual update. It attains
$\mathcal O(\epsilon^{-5/2})$ complexity with a fixed penalty, matching, to the
best of our knowledge, the fastest multi-loop rate for this problem class
without inner solvers or dual projections.
}
